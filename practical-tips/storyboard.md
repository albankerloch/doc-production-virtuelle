# Storyboard

**Define the shot :**

* Where is the camera
* Does the camera moves&#x20;
* For how long
* What is the frame
* What is the expecting lighting
* What accessories is needed
* What clothes is nedded



**Prepare a time reminder for the shots :**&#x20;

* Create a diagram on diagram.io
* List a shot by page
* List shots with the same scene and angle down
* List shots with another scene and angle forward
* Specify the time and camera movement of each shot
* Provide a screenshot previsualisation of each shot

**Prepare frame calcs :**&#x20;

* Open DaVinci Resolve
* Import the reference shots on a timeline with a  3187 x 1080 resolution
* Strech the reference shots to fit the timeline (unlock zoom x and y)
* Put the viewer at 16 %
* Cut a 15.2 cm x 5.1 cm piece of calc
* Draw the frame on the calc on the computer screen
