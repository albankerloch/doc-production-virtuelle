# Shooting Process

**Camera and Gimbal**

* Plug the Camera with the false battery to electricity
* Plug the Camera micro-HDMI port to the Video Assist
* Mount the Camera to the RS4 Pro
* Mount the focus controller motors and USB cables to the RS4 Pro
* Balance the RS4 Pro
* Turn the RS4 Pro on
* Swith it to Custom Mode (no movement on any axis)



**Camera Movement**

* If needed (slider) :&#x20;
  * Set Point A (left when facing the controller)
  * Set Point B
  * Set the mode to Cycles (use the power button to switch type from Once)
  * Test the movement
  * Time the movement (especially for camera movement)
* If needed (camera movement) :
  * Connect the RS4 Pro to the iPhone
  * Go to Create
  * Choose Suivi
  * Set positions and time
  * Test the movement
  * &#x20;Swith to Custom Mode (no movement on any axis)



**Framing and Lighting**

* Set the dummy on the scene
* Adjust the camera position and the frame
* Remove 3D marker half-hidden by the dummy on the green screen
* Adjust the focus
* Set the White Balance (use Auto then find the mode closest to Auto to fix the value)
* Set up the ligth with the dummy
* Move the Video Assit close to me and in my field of vision at the first frame



**Shooting**

* In that order, launch :
  * the slider (if needed)
  * the RS4 movement (if needed, on the app)
  * the recording on the Video Assist
* Act :)
* Stop the recording on the Video Assist



**QA (Quality Assessment)**

* Move the Video Assist next to the desk
* Plug the Video Assist to the screen
* Review the shot
* If the shot is good :&#x20;
  * Place the Color Checker Video at the subject place
  * Record a very short video
  * Change the shot number on the Video Assist
* If the shot is not good :
  * Write down what was wrong
  * Change the scene number on the Video Assist



**Immediatly after the shooting, launch the footage backup process**
