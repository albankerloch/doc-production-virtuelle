# Ground level with lines

**Set the X axis and the scale**

On the Lens room, click on Add Line on the left panel

Draw a line on a frame of the shot that has good lines

For the X axis, draw from the left to the right

Chose "on X axis with Length"

Enter the Length on the left panel

**Set the Y axis**

click on Add Line on the left panel

For the Y axis, draw from the foreground to the bottom

Chose "on Y axis"

**Set other lines as guides**

click on Add Line on the left panel

For the Z axis, draw vertically

Chose "Parrallel to the Z axis"

Draw other lines as guides ("Parrallel to the Z axis" / "Parrallel to the X axis" / "Parrallel to the Y axis")

Click on Align on the left panel
