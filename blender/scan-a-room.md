# LiDAR scan with Polycam

**Polycam (iPhone)**

* Use the + button to start a new scan
* Choose Lidar as option
* Scan the room at every height
* Do S pattern path to scan the whole room
* Choose the second to last option to process the scan (the last will use cloud and after you'll need the Pro version to download it ...)
* Download it in .obj
* Send it by mail to have it on your computer
